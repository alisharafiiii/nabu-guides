# nabuguides project rules

## X article covers

These rules apply whenever creating or editing a cover for an X Article.

- Use an exact **5:2** canvas. Preferred working size: **1500 × 600 px**.
- Design for the X mobile app first. The app places phone-status UI across the top and circular controls over the left and right sides of the cover.
- Keep the top **18%** free of essential text, faces, logos, and small details.
- Treat the outer **13%** on both the left and right as collision zones, especially from **18% to 58%** of the canvas height.
- Keep all essential content inside the primary safe area: **x = 15–85%**, **y = 20–82%**.
- Give headlines extra right-side breathing room. A title must never extend into the right control zone.
- Limit the headline to two short lines and roughly 55% of the canvas width. Prefer larger type with fewer words.
- Decorative elements may enter the collision zones only when losing or covering them does not change the meaning.
- Do not stretch one design to serve both X and the website tile. Create a separate crop or composition when their safe areas conflict.
- Before delivery, inspect the cover with `assets/templates/x-article-cover-safe-area.svg` overlaid and verify a mobile-sized preview.

The recurring design lesson is: **the cover is not edge-to-edge usable space on mobile**. Compose around X's interface, not underneath it.
